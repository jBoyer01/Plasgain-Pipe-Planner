07 / Load map

Selected fleet load plan

Prototype: every stacked layer now requires a flat full-pack platform directly below it with equal-or-greater supporting width. Mixed-height layers are top-only, loose pipe cannot sit on the trailer floor, 600 packs are limited to three-high, usable load height is capped at 2,800 mm (allowing for a 1.5 m deck within a 4.3 m overall planning height), the packer prefers loading bigger diameters before smaller ones where a safe layout allows it, and customer-delivery fleet options rank 14 Tonne/Semi combinations ahead of B-Double/A-Double combinations. Nesting can be disabled for comparison or non-nested planning, and displayed package/truck weights include all nested pipe carried in that package. Physical validation, payload/axle rules, restraint/stability checks and historical load validation are still required.
